THIN ICE — The sea ice is disappearing

Open index.html in a browser.

FILES:
index.html — content and page sections
favicon.svg — tab icon (an ice floe)
styles.css — layout, colours and animations
script.js — all interactions (melting background, snow, cracking ice and the splitting floe, Arctic map 1979–2026 with the polar bear, trend chart, sunlight mirror, the melting loop, glass of water, animal cards)
DESIGN.md — design direction: concept, colours, fonts, interactions, motion

To change the yearly ice figures (SEPT: every September 1979–2026 from the NSIDC Sea Ice Index v4.0), the animals or the actions, edit the data at the top of script.js.

This is a student project. The Arctic map is an illustration: the ice area is drawn to scale with the real September extent, but the shape is not the real coastline. Figures are rounded; sources are listed in the footer.

All drawings (polar bear, seal, walrus, cod, ice algae, danger icons) are original and drawn in SVG at the top of index.html.
